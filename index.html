Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ResumeCraft

Is ResumeCraft free?
Yes. ResumeCraft is designed as a free frontend-first resume builder. All features are available at no cost.
Is my resume saved?
Yes. Resume data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Your data stays on your device and is never sent to external servers.
Can I create multiple resumes?
Yes. You can create, edit, and manage multiple resumes for different job applications.
Can I customize templates?
Yes. You can customize accent colors, fonts, font sizes, and section spacing to match your preferences.
